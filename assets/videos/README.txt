Tips for future clips:
- Keep 15-60s
- 720p is plenty
- Use clear, slow enough actions so the flow is understandable without audio
- Consider adding a 1-2s title frame or use poster attribute on <video>

For Google Sites hosting later: YouTube embeds may be easier than direct large video hosting, but the current website intentionally uses local MP4s so browser-native video controls remain available.
